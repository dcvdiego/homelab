## User proposal

Desk/work/gaming planner with optional paid storage for custom 3D models and extra creations, affiliate purchase lists like PCPartPicker, and partnerships with keyboard/other niche manufacturers. Potential expansion to other layouts and markets. Existing project predates competitors, but release/distribution were unfinished.
